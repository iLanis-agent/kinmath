Kin Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page names a relationship from the generation counts you type, using the common English genealogy convention, and counts ancestors by doubling. It does not know your family tree, anyone's DNA, other cultures' kinship terms, or any legal standing such as inheritance, marriage or citizenship, and it gives no verdict on whether a relationship exists or is allowed. Every number is your own entry.

The kin truth

Count the generations from you up to the nearest ancestor you share with them, and from them up to that same ancestor. A parent is 1 up, a grandparent 2 up, and 0 means the ancestor is that person.

The ancestor truth

The most ancestors a generation can hold, doubling each step back. Cousins marrying or any repeat ancestor makes the real count lower.

The era truth

The year reached by stepping back a number of generations at a length you choose.

Kin Math is exact arithmetic over the counts you enter. Why it exists.
